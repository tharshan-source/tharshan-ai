The Unsplash Licence allows free commercial and non-commercial use without permission or attribution, but not selling unaltered copies or building a competing image service. Credit is given here as good practice.

Checks before adding a photo:

- No visible brand names, logos or readable personal data (number plates, faces in close-up, screens).
- People only at a distance, from above or partly out of frame; never posed.
- A licence that allows commercial use with modification.
